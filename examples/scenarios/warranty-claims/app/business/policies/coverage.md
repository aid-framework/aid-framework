# Coverage: what the manufacturer pays for

## Base warranty

The base warranty covers any defect in materials or workmanship for **4 years or
80,000 km**, whichever comes first. A defect is a departure from the published build
specification that the owner could not reasonably have caused or prevented.

The base warranty travels with the vehicle, not with the owner. A claim is assessed against
the vehicle's registration date and odometer, not against the date of purchase by the current
owner.

## Powertrain extension

Battery electric and hybrid vehicles carry a powertrain extension to **8 years or 160,000 km**
on the traction motor, inverter, reduction gear and high-voltage battery enclosure. The
extension covers the named assemblies only. It does not extend the base warranty to any other
component, and it does not extend the consequential-damage position in WC-EXC-002.

## Safety campaigns take precedence

Where an open safety campaign exists for the same system as the reported symptom, the campaign
repair is performed regardless of warranty status, mileage or age. The campaign is not a
goodwill act and is not coded against the warranty budget. See WC-CAM-003.

## Goodwill

A repair outside coverage may still be performed as goodwill at the regional manager's
discretion. Goodwill is a commercial decision, not a warranty determination: it is recorded
against the claim, it requires an approval before any part is ordered, and it never appears in
a determination as `approve`.

## What a determination must carry

Every determination names the clause it relied on. A determination that cites no clause is
returned to the adjudicator, because a reviewer cannot release a decision they cannot check.
