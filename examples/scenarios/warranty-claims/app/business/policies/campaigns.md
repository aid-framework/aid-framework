# Safety campaigns

## Precedence over warranty

A safety campaign is a manufacturer recall. Where an open campaign covers the same system as
the reported symptom, the campaign repair is authorised regardless of warranty status,
mileage or vehicle age, and the claim is coded against the campaign rather than the warranty
budget. The campaign outranks every other clause in this corpus, including the consequential
damage exclusion.

## Interaction with a warranty claim

A single visit can produce both a campaign repair and a warranty repair. The campaign part is
ordered through the campaign channel; the warranty part follows the ordinary approval path.
Filing the campaign part as a warranty repair understates campaign completion and is a
reporting defect, not an adjudication choice.

## When the two disagree

If the campaign says a component is defective and the reported symptom points somewhere else,
the campaign still runs. The adjudicator does not get to decide that the campaign is wrong;
a report that the campaign does not address the reported symptom goes to the region, which is
what the `escalate` path exists for.
